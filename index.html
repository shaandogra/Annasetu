Why AnnaSetu Is the Winning 24-Hour Prototype

AnnaSetu bridges time-critical logistics with deterministic safety. Unlike unconstrained AI chatbots that risk hallucinating food safety claims, AnnaSetu enforces an atomic state-machine with 4-digit physical verification codes, auto-expiry calculation, and distance-weighted matching.

1

The Kitchen Reality

Banquet halls discard surplus food at 11:30 PM because existing directories are static phonebooks without real-time expiry awareness.

2

Urgency-Ranked Match

Nearby Asha Shelter gets an immediate notification. The matching engine ranks by remaining shelf-life and proximity (1.8 km).

3

Atomic Claim & PIN

NGO reserves the food. The listing is instantly locked from other NGOs, and a secure 4-digit handover PIN (e.g., 7419) is issued.

4

Physical Verification

Driver arrives at kitchen. Donor inputs PIN to mark status as "Completed". The transaction is permanently logged in the audit trail.

5

Role Auth & Email Verification

Single-page portal for Donors & NGOs with backend user verification, error handling for wrong credentials, password recovery OTP, and live email verification.
